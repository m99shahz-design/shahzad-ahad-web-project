Behind the Scenes: How We Choose Fabrics

IN BRAND NEWS • Dec 05, 2025

Every piece in the Shahz&Ahd collection starts with fabric. Before we think about cuts, colours or details, we test how a material feels, moves and survives everyday wear.

We look for softness, durability and structure. The fabric has to feel good on the skin, hold its shape after multiple washes and still look sharp after a long day of use.

Our team runs wash tests, stretch tests and real-life trials — wearing samples to class, work, and hangouts. If a fabric pills too fast, loses its colour or feels rough, it doesn’t make the cut.

This process takes longer, but it’s worth it. It’s how we make sure the hoodie you love today still feels great months from now.

For us, quality isn’t a marketing word. It’s a decision at every step: from fabric rolls to the final piece you add to your wardrobe.
