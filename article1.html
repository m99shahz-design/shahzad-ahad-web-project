Building a Simple Capsule Wardrobe

IN LIFESTYLE • Jan 10, 2026

A capsule wardrobe is all about doing more with less. Instead of owning dozens of items you never wear, you focus on a smaller selection of pieces that work together in many different ways.

Start with the basics: a black t-shirt, clean white tee, neutral hoodie, one good pair of jeans and a jacket that works for both casual and slightly dressier settings. Choose colours that mix easily, like black, white, grey, beige and navy.

From there, you can add a few seasonal pieces — a light overshirt for spring, a puffer jacket for winter, or a statement cap. These items keep your outfits feeling fresh without needing a huge wardrobe.

Accessories are your secret weapon. A cap, sunglasses or simple chain can switch the vibe of an outfit instantly. You don’t need a lot; you just need the right ones.

The goal of a capsule wardrobe isn’t to be boring, it’s to make getting dressed easier and more intentional. Less time choosing outfits, more time actually living your day.
