3 Easy Layering Formulas for Busy Days

IN STYLE TIPS • Jan 03, 2026

Layering is one of the easiest ways to make your outfits look intentional and stay comfortable throughout the day. When done right, it adds depth and structure without feeling heavy or bulky.

Formula 1: T-shirt → Hoodie → Overshirt
This combo is perfect for everyday wear. Start with a basic tee, add a comfortable hoodie, then throw on an overshirt or light jacket. You can remove layers as the day warms up.

Formula 2: Long-sleeve Tee → Puffer Vest → Jacket
Great for cooler days when you need warmth but still want freedom of movement. The vest keeps your core warm while your arms stay more flexible.

Formula 3: Basic Tee → Knit Sweater → Coat
Simple, clean and slightly dressier. This works well for dinners, meetups or days when you want a more polished look.

In all three formulas, stick to a simple colour palette. That way, each layer blends smoothly and you never feel over-styled.
